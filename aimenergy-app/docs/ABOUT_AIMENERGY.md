# About AimEnergy

## Built for energy access, starting with Uganda

AimEnergy is a browser-based mini-grid planning and feasibility prototype for developers, energy planners, analysts and field teams. It connects village evidence, inspectable demand assumptions and exploratory system scenarios, helping teams decide what to investigate before handing a project to detailed engineering.

### Understand demand before choosing equipment

Explore 411 real Lamwo village records, inspect household model evidence, and build explicit household, business and public-service scenarios. Compare 24 months of assumed growth and retain the source of each input.

### Test an hourly system scenario

Explore 8,760 hours of PV, battery and generator dispatch. Change capacities and operating assumptions, inspect unmet demand and curtailment, and see how a proposed system responds to the demand scenario. Results are exploratory; battery ageing, grid exchange and detailed engineering are not modeled.

### Keep financial assumptions visible

Review illustrative lifecycle costs, LCOE, NPV and annual cashflows. Save local versions and export the complete scenario or a print-ready report. Costs are editable assumptions, not quotations or investment conclusions. Versions stay on the device; account storage and cross-device synchronization are not implemented.

### Bring the field into the assessment

Prepare the app for offline use, record field responses and compare revisions while preserving the baseline. Preview basic-phone participation through a labelled USSD simulator; completed simulations remain separate from real observations and measured demand.

### Use traceable solar evidence

A real PVGIS solar series loads automatically for the Orii Gangodye example. Other sites can use matching PVGIS files, with location and hourly-coverage checks. Satellite/drone context and dated survey imports support assessment; imagery does not measure electricity use.

AimEnergy supports a transparent pre-investment workflow. It does not currently produce cable designs, LV/MV networks, protection studies, single-line diagrams, certified equipment sizing or lender-ready due diligence. Its aim is to make assumptions and evidence gaps easier to examine before those decisions.
